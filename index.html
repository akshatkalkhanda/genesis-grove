WELCOME, WORLDMAKER

Small beginnings.
Endless possibilities.

Paint land with Shape, choose a people under Civilizations, add animals under Creatures, then stir things up with Powers.

Every kingdom has its own banner color. Ore veins grow naturally in hills or can be placed with Powers. Farmers, woodcutters, miners, smiths, and builders keep towns growing; research spends ore to unlock new ages. Barracks train soldiers to defend towns and fight wars. Peaceful kingdoms send supplies by caravan or ship. Open Kingdoms to inspect jobs, armies, supplies, trade, and diplomacy. The new-world menu has a 100-kingdom, 1,000-year challenge.
