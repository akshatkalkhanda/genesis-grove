# WorldBox video comparison

## Colorful world and species reference, 19 October 2026

The supplied [eight-kingdom video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dwz6yEqh9B4),
its transcript, and the user's screenshots show animal peoples in ice, maple,
savanna, desert, jungle, mushroom, crystal, and magical regions. The map in the
video is a handcrafted community map. Version 0.10.0 adds original procedural
biomes, eleven peoples, eighteen wildlife species, and a portrait inspection card.
Prismatic Wildlands starts eight peoples, with habitat preferences affecting food.
It does not reproduce the community map or WorldBox artwork. Genetics, families,
languages, subspecies, and the full reference game's roster are not implemented.

## Earlier challenge reference

Reference: [Mellon's 100 kingdoms / 1,000 years video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f4EIP_n-6Ng).
Its description says the run spawns 100 kingdoms, disables rebellions, and follows survival over 1,000 years. Sampled frames around 10 and 30 minutes show a crowded, color-coded map across several landmasses, multiple cities per kingdom, ships, and kingdom statistics. The video has no available transcript, so this comparison is limited to those observed elements.

| Element | Genesis Grove before | Genesis Grove now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starting kingdoms | Three; eight total | 100 founders in a dedicated challenge; 120 total capacity |
| Long run | 20 simulation seconds per year; up to 5× speed | Challenge years last two simulation seconds; 20× speed and year 100/250/500/1,000 chronicle milestones |
| Survival | Live village count | Founding-kingdom survival counter and population-ranked village list |
| Diplomacy | Manual alliance, peace, and war | Optional autonomous neighboring diplomacy; manual controls still available |
| Rebellions | None | World-law toggle, off by default; enabled law can split a populous mature town |
| Map | Islands or continent | Original four-landmass challenge map, with color-coded kingdom territories |
| Settlements | One village per kingdom | Kingdoms can found additional towns with caravan or ship settlers |
| War | Local fighting and raids | Local fighting plus town and capital sieges that transfer ownership |
| Kingdom details | Population list | Profile with population, towns, resources, deaths, alliances, and wars |
| Kingdom identity | One race color shared by same-race kingdoms | Unique kingdom banners, stronger territory colors, and matching kingdom cards |
| Creatures | Similar tiny animal marks | Species-specific pixel silhouettes and selection icons |
| Ages and weapons | Decorative age names | Fourteen ages through Cosmic Dust, distinct equipment, ranged fire, and energy beams |

The challenge uses Genesis Grove's own terrain, art, and simulation. It does not reproduce the video's downloadable workshop map or the full WorldBox mechanics. Ships carry settlers, while battles remain local to land and naval invasions are not simulated.

Version 0.12.0 also adds working roads and transports, monuments and cosmic institutions, original larger tree sprites, angels and demons, persistent flags, rulers, and succession. These extend Genesis Grove with its own fantasy technology; they do not claim WorldBox feature parity.
